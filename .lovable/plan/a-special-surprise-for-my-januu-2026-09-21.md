# A Special Surprise for My Januu

## Goal
Build a single-page, mobile-first birthday journey from Uzan Khan to My Januu, using the exact romantic pink, lavender, gold, cursive direction and the case-sensitive password `for-you-i-will`.

## Experience
- Create eight smooth full-screen chapters: welcome, lock, birthday wish, cake, memories, fun zone, love letter, and finale.
- Add a persistent heart progress trail, back navigation, music controls, floating petals/hearts, sparkles, shooting stars, click ripples, and celebratory bursts.
- Start sound only after the opening click. Use lightweight synthesized background music and effects so the experience works immediately without copyrighted or missing audio files.
- Include the October 1 countdown, gift box, ten love reasons, memory jar, and secret hover/tap message.

## Interactions
- Enforce the exact password with playful success and error states.
- Make all three candles individually extinguishable, then enable the animated cake cut and first-slice reveal.
- Provide polished memory placeholders that clearly invite future personal photos without broken imagery.
- Build all five activities: a five-question quiz, three scratch cards, love meter, five balloon pops, and three emoji guesses.
- Animate the envelope opening and letter reveal, then finish with a full celebration and replay option.

## Visual and Accessibility Details
- Use semantic pink, lavender, gold, rose, ink, and paper tokens with Dancing Script, Playfair Display, and Poppins.
- Keep motion GPU-friendly, limit background particle counts on smaller screens, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Support touch, mouse, and keyboard controls with readable contrast, clear focus states, stable layouts, and no overlapping controls.

## Validation
- Check the complete journey at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Verify the password, candles, cake, games, letter, back navigation, audio controls, countdown, and replay behavior.
